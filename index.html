About Us

More than music. It's a whole vibe.
Centennial Phonk Club is a student-driven community built around phonk culture, creative edits, memes, and pure hype. What started as an appreciation for the culture is becoming a space for students to share their interests, express their creativity, and connect over the things they enjoy.
Whether you're here for the music, the edits, or just the memes, you're part of the vibe.
